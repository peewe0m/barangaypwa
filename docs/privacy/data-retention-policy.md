# Data Retention Policy

Status: Draft for barangay review and approval  
System: Barangay Management System  
Owner: Barangay Darasa  
Last reviewed: 2026-05-28

## Purpose
This policy defines how long system records should be retained, who may authorize disposal, and how disposal should be documented. Retention should follow the Data Privacy Act principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality, while preserving records required for official barangay operations.

## Scope
Applies to database records, uploaded files, generated PDFs, exports, backups, and audit logs handled by the Barangay Management System.

## Retention Schedule
| Record type | Default retention | Disposal trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resident and household master records | Active residency plus 10 years | Resident no longer served and no pending official purpose |
| Barangay certificates, clearances, IDs, and request records | 10 years after issuance or denial | End of retention and no active dispute/audit |
| Public portal submissions and applicant photos | 1 year after completion if not part of an issued official record | Request completed, cancelled, or denied |
| Business clearance records | 10 years after final transaction | End of retention and no pending inspection/audit |
| Blotter and incident records | 15 years or longer if legally required | Written authorization from barangay leadership/legal adviser |
| Health, welfare, and sensitive assistance records | 10 years after final service action | End of retention and no active case/referral |
| Payment records | 10 years | End of retention and no pending audit |
| User accounts | Employment/assignment duration plus 1 year | Account disabled and access review complete |
| Audit logs | Minimum 2 years, recommended 5 years | Security review complete and no investigation pending |
| Generated exports | Delete within 30 days unless officially filed | Export purpose completed |
| Backups | Rolling 30-90 days, plus approved monthly archive if needed | Backup rotation expires |

## Disposal Procedure
1. Identify records that reached the retention period.
2. Confirm there is no pending audit, legal hold, complaint, investigation, or active service need.
3. Obtain written approval from the Barangay Captain or authorized records officer.
4. Delete electronic records using application workflows or approved database/storage tools.
5. Delete associated uploaded files, generated PDFs, and cached exports.
6. Record the disposal date, record category, date range, approver, and staff member who performed disposal.

## Legal Holds
When a complaint, audit, investigation, court action, or official records request is pending, disposal must stop for affected records until the hold is lifted in writing.

## Backups
Deleted production records may remain in backup archives until the backup expires through normal rotation. Backups must not be restored for routine lookup of disposed records unless authorized for incident recovery, audit, or legal need.

## Review
Review this policy annually and whenever legal requirements, local ordinances, or barangay recordkeeping rules change.
