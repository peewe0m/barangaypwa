# Role and Action Permission SOP + Staff Training

Status: Draft for barangay review and approval  
System: Barangay Management System  
Owner: Barangay Darasa  
Last reviewed: 2026-05-28

## Purpose
This SOP defines how user accounts, module permissions, and action permissions are requested, approved, reviewed, and removed.

## Permission Principles
- Use named accounts only; shared staff accounts are not allowed.
- Grant the least access required for the staff member's current duties.
- Admin accounts may access all modules and are the only accounts allowed to delete records.
- Staff accounts should receive module and action permissions based on their assigned work.
- Disable accounts immediately when staff leave or no longer need system access.

## Account Request Procedure
1. Supervisor requests access with staff name, email, role, modules, and allowed actions.
2. Barangay Captain, Secretary, or authorized officer approves access.
3. Admin creates the account in Settings.
4. Staff sets or receives a temporary password and changes it as required.
5. Admin records the approval date and access reason.

## Permission Change Procedure
1. Requestor documents the needed change and reason.
2. Approver confirms the change is required for assigned duties.
3. Admin updates role, module permissions, or action permissions.
4. Staff confirms they can perform required tasks.
5. Admin reviews audit logs if the change was urgent or high-risk.

## Offboarding Procedure
1. Disable the user account on the last working day or immediately after reassignment.
2. Rotate shared operational secrets if the staff member had access outside the app.
3. Review recent audit logs for unusual activity.
4. Preserve records created by the account; do not delete official records solely because the staff member left.

## Quarterly Access Review
Every quarter, an admin and authorized officer must review:
- Active accounts
- Disabled/inactive accounts
- Admin accounts
- Module permissions
- Delete/export/approval permissions
- Recent audit logs for high-risk actions

Record the review date, reviewer, issues found, and actions taken.

## Staff Training Topics
- Data Privacy Act basics and data subject rights
- Only collect data needed for the barangay service
- Do not browse resident records without official purpose
- Do not share passwords or leave sessions unlocked
- Verify identity before releasing documents
- Use exports only for approved official work
- Report suspected wrong access, lost files, or suspicious activity immediately
- Follow retention, backup, and disaster recovery procedures

## Training Schedule
- New staff: before account activation or within the first week
- Existing staff: annually
- Admin users: annually plus after major permission/audit changes
